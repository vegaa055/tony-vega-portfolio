Built for the love of the subject matter as much as the engineering.

## What it shows

Live readouts from the Space Weather Prediction Center: solar wind speed and density, the interplanetary magnetic field, the Kp geomagnetic index, GOES X-ray flux, proton and electron flux at GOES altitudes, aurora probability, and current solar imagery.

## Why this aesthetic

Space weather data deserves to look like it's being displayed inside a real operations center. CRT scanlines, amber-on-black panels, blinking status lights. Pure affection dressed up as UI.

## Stack

React, Vite, Chart.js, and the NOAA SWPC public JSON feeds.
